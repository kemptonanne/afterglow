# Bring the journal look to the main app and live site

1. Accept the "Journal restyle" draft into the main app. This brings over the new paper-white and sage look, the new fonts, the tidy one-column wall, search behind the header icon, and the compact "+ Save a moment" button.
2. The draft's four sample moments stay behind. They lived only in the draft's test data, so your real moments are unaffected.
3. After the draft is accepted, check the main app at phone size: the wall, opening a moment, search, and the Save a moment screen.
4. Publish again so the live site at afterglow-moments.lovable.app gets the new look. If you added Afterglow to your home screen, it updates on its own.

## Rollback
If you don't like it, you can go back to the earlier version from History. Then publish again to restore the old look on the live site.

## Technical notes
- The draft is accepted with drafts--accept, which runs after the turn ends. The phone-size check and the publish happen in the next turn, once the accept has gone through.
- The draft's only known leftover: the "My wall" tab isn't underlined while search is open. It is left as is unless you ask.
